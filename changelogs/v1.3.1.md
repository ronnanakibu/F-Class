# Release v1.3.1 — Mobile Gallery Showcase & Audio Engine Polish

## Bug Fixes & Improvements

- **Asset Integrity & Zero 404s**:
  - Restored and automatically generated all high-resolution cybernetic artwork assets for Project Showcases, Gallery Slides, and Mobile Stories.
  - Eliminated all missing resource network errors across the application.
- **Mobile Interactive Gallery Showcase Refinement**:
  - Completely redesigned the mobile layout of the showcase card with increased vertical headroom and responsive typography.
  - Eliminated title duplication and text overlap on small screens (e.g. iPhone and Android devices).
  - Multi-stage background vignettes and contrast overlays now ensure clear, crisp readability of all milestone stories and tags.
- **Seamless Audio Volume Adjustment**:
  - Decoupled the volume and mute controls from the audio playback lifecycle.
  - Users can now smoothly slide the volume up and down during playback without audio interruptions, unexpected pausing, or restarting the song.
- **Performance & Animation Optimization**:
  - Migrated the audio frequency equalizer to GPU-accelerated CSS transform keyframes, eliminating layout recalculations and ensuring smooth 60fps rendering.
  - Optimized gallery slideshow progress updates to reduce CPU and battery consumption on mobile devices.
