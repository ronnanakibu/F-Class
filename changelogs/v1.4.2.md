# Release v1.4.2 — Multi-Provider Music Selector & Interactive 30-Second Audio Trimmer

## New Features & Enhancements

- **Multi-Provider Music Search Console**:
  - Added an intuitive provider selector in the Admin song modal with 3 flexible options: **Apple Music**, **YouTube**, and **Direct File Upload**.
  - Allows searching directly by song title or artist without needing to manually copy-paste external video links.

- **Direct YouTube Metadata Engine**:
  - Automatically fetches real-time video titles, artist/channel credits, high-resolution artwork thumbnails, and exact durations directly from search keywords.

- **Interactive 30-Second Audio Trimmer**:
  - Introduced a precision audio selection card for YouTube tracks with an interactive range slider to pick any 30-second snippet (such as the chorus or bridge).
  - Includes live start/end time markers (`MM:SS`) and 1-click preset jumps: *Awal (00:00)*, *Bait 1 (00:30)*, *Reff 1 (01:00)*, *Reff 2 (01:30)*, and *Bridge (02:00)*.

- **Direct Local Audio File Upload**:
  - Enables uploading custom audio files (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.wav`) directly from desktop or mobile devices for custom anthems.
