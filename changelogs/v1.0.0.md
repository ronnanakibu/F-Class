# Release v1.0.0 — Enhanced Prompt (Production Version)

## Features & Highlights

- **Interactive Memory Scrapbook Engine**: Blends Spotify Live Lyrics, Apple Music Sing, and a nostalgic Digital Polaroid Scrapbook synchronized with the song "Feels Like We're Still a Lover" by Aldi Haqq.
- **Data-Driven Story System**: Directed by a unified `story.json` timeline controlling lyrics, photos, handwritten notes, scene transitions, emotions, and camera movements.
- **GPU-Friendly Lyric Synchronization**: Fast 60fps lyric motion using container `translateY()` transform shifts instead of expensive layout-thrashing scroll elements. Active lyric glows with warmth while inactive lyrics blur smoothly.
- **Layered Ambient Background Engine**: 6 composited layers (Base canvas color, SVG noise grain, emotion-driven radial bloom, light leak, Canvas particle dust, and vignette).
- **Physics-Based Polaroid Photo Stack**: Photo cards appear on timeline triggers with spring physics, masking tape decorations, paper shadow, and handwritten captions. Tapping opens a fullscreen photo viewer modal with backdrop blur.
- **Cinematic Virtual Camera System**: Subtle viewport zoom, drift, and camera-shake spring physics responsive to song sections and beat impacts.
- **Secret Diary Messages**: Handwritten notes in Caveat font reveal beneath active lyrics with a 600ms spring blur-in transition.
- **Apple-Inspired Audio Player**: Minimal floating glassmorphism player with circular ambient wave glow around the play button (driven by AudioContext RMS analysis), progress bar, volume controls, and replay functionality.
- **Premium Preloader**: Smooth "Loading Memories..." preloader with progress tracking before fading into the intro scene.
