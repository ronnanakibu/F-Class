# Release v1.1.0 — Apple-Style Showcase & Perpetual Story Archive

## Features & Highlights

- **Apple-Style Interactive Gallery Slideshow**: Replaced the static milestone timeline with an Apple Keynote-inspired cinematic showcase featuring fluid transitions, photo and looping video support, and segmented animated progress indicators that fill smoothly over time.
- **Interactive Playback & Gestures**: Supports play/pause toggling, next/previous navigation, slide jumping via progress indicators, hold-to-pause, mobile swipe gestures, and a fullscreen lightbox view.
- **Perpetual Instagram Stories Archive**: Stories posted by the class account `@comeinone.f` are permanently archived in a dedicated time-capsule, preserving memories without the 24-hour expiration limit.
- **Authentic Instagram Story Viewer**: An interactive overlay viewer featuring top segmented progress bars, profile header with verified badge, tap left/right navigation, press-and-hold to pause and hide controls, video audio controls, and an interactive heart reaction with floating particle effects.
- **Dedicated Asset Organization**: Structured asset folders (`public/gallery/` for class milestones and `public/stories/` for archived stories) to keep the project clean and maintainable.
- **Unified Vault & Story Masonry Grid**: Seamlessly merged the Instagram stories archive with The Vault's masonry card layout, featuring category badges and timestamps in the bottom-left corner of each card, category filter tabs, and direct fullscreen story playback on card tap.
- **Featured Developer Showcase Projects**: Highlighted prominent engineering projects in What We Build, including EnglishQuest (interactive English learning web app) and WhatsApp Bots (WABOT2.0 multi-device automation engine), complete with mockup banners, direct live demo buttons, and custom folder management in public/projects/.
- **Automated Bot Ingestion & Admin Portal**: Equipped with an automated ingestion endpoint for WhatsApp Bot (WABOT) to forward media directly, along with a dedicated Story Management tab in the Admin Console for manual uploads, previewing, and archiving.
